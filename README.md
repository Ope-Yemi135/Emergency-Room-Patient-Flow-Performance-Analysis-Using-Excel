# Emergency Room Patient Flow and Performance Analysis Using Excel
## Project Background

The hospital is experiencing a high number of patient visits on a daily basis. The goal is help the management seeks to better understand of patient flow, waiting times, admission outcomes, and overall patient experience in order to improve service delivery and operational efficiency.

---

## Project Objectives
### Management wants to understand:
- The total volume of patients visiting the ER
- Average, minimum, and maximum waiting times
- How waiting time varies by age group
- Admission status and its relationship to patient satisfaction
- Demographic patterns (age, gender, race)
- Which departments receive the most referrals
 Areas where service quality and efficiency can be improved

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
### The dashboard highlights the following metrics:
- Total Patient Visits
- Average Patient Waiting Time
- Maximum Patient Waiting Time
- Minimum Patient Waiting Time
- Average Patient Satisfaction Score

---

## Analytical Approach
### Using Microsoft Excel, the following steps were taken:
- Data cleaning and validation
- Categorization of patients by age, gender, race, and admission status
- Creation of pivot tables for aggregation
- Visualization using charts and dashboard elements
- Interpretation of trends to support decision-making

---

## Key Findings
- Adult patients account for the largest proportion of ER visits
- Waiting times are highest among adults and children
- Admission and non-admission rates are nearly equal
- Patient satisfaction is slightly higher among non-admitted patients
- A large number of patients did not require departmental referral, suggesting ER overcrowding
- Gender distribution of visits is relatively balanced

---

## Insights
- 50% of patients are admitted  which is very high for an Emergency Room.
- Most patients come by themselves , not referred.
- Wait times are moderate (longest 60 min), but patient satisfaction is very low.
- Demographics show that male gender visit more, and they are mostly  White, and also the highest visits are from adult-aged  (20–60).

---

## Recommendation
- Strengthen primary care for managing non-emergency cases.
- Check admission requirements to lower avoidable admissions.
- initiate Observation Units for temporary periods of observation
- Establish fast-track lanes for minor injuries/illness.
- Strengthen health campaigns in ages 41–65 years (chronic disease; CVD;
- Monitor admission rates by race and genders.
- Educate ER personnel on communication, patient comfort, and empathy

---

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Charts & Visualizations
- Dashboard Design
- Data Aggregation

---

## Conclusion

This Excel dashboard provides management with a clear, data-driven view of ER operations. By leveraging these insights, hospital leadership can make informed decisions to improve patient flow, reduce waiting times, and enhance overall emergency care delivery.
